What this can and can't tell you

Averages, not households

Results are neighbourhood averages from banded census incomes. Household-level results need Stats NZ's restricted IDI data or Treasury's tax-benefit model.

Who pays the land tax

Owner-occupiers pay on their homes and farmers on their farms. Renters pay none: research on land taxes finds they aren't passed on to tenants without rent control. Tax on rentals and business land falls on owners and shareholders.

Not modelled

Child, sole-parent, disability and housing top-ups; KiwiSaver 2.0; land prices falling 10–15% as TOP expects; any change in how much people work. Superannuitants can defer the land tax until sale.
